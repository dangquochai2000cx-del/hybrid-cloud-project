> During my participation in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, I had the opportunity to experience a practical working environment, explore new technologies, and apply my academic knowledge to project implementation. The following are my comments, experiences, and suggestions to help improve the quality of the internship program.

### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The internship environment at FCAJ gave me the opportunity to become familiar with a more professional and proactive working style. I was able to explore new technologies, practice independently, and solve problems that arose during project implementation. This was a valuable experience that helped me become more familiar with a real-world working environment.

**2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team**

The guidance and support provided during the internship helped me establish a clearer direction when studying technical topics. In particular, having the opportunity to conduct independent research, practice, and troubleshoot problems helped me gain a deeper understanding of the technologies being implemented. I also had the opportunity to develop my self-learning skills and ability to solve problems independently.

**3. Relevance of the Work to My Field of Study**

The internship activities were closely related to my major in Computer Networks and Data Communications. While working on the Hybrid Cloud project, I applied my knowledge of computer networking, routing, network device configuration, and Site-to-Site VPN connectivity. At the same time, I gained exposure to AWS cloud services and system monitoring tools.

**4. Learning Opportunities and Skills Development**

The internship provided me with opportunities to explore and practice various technologies, including Amazon VPC, EC2, Site-to-Site VPN, Prometheus, and Grafana. In addition to technical knowledge, I improved my ability to search for technical documentation, understand technical guides, troubleshoot errors, and document implementation procedures. These skills will serve as a valuable foundation for my future career.

**5. Company Culture and Teamwork**

Participating in the program helped me better understand the importance of teamwork and communication in the workplace. Sharing knowledge, experiences, and providing mutual support helped make the learning and practical activities more effective. I also realized that the ability to collaborate with others is an essential skill in the information technology industry.

**6. Policies and Benefits for Interns**

I appreciate the learning opportunities, practical experience, and exposure to real-world knowledge provided by the FCAJ program. The opportunity to explore technologies currently used in cloud computing has helped me gain a clearer direction for my professional development. I hope the program will continue maintaining and expanding its support activities for future interns.

### Additional Questions

**1. What did I appreciate most during the internship?**

What I appreciated most was the opportunity to independently research and implement a practical Hybrid Cloud model. Through configuring AWS network infrastructure, establishing Site-to-Site VPN connections, and building a monitoring system using Prometheus and Grafana, I gained a better understanding of how computer networking knowledge can be integrated with cloud computing technologies.

**2. What do I think the program should improve for future interns?**

I hope the program can provide more detailed documentation and step-by-step guidance for each stage, along with practical examples to help interns follow and complete their tasks more easily. In addition, organizing more technical discussions and periodic progress reviews would help interns receive feedback and adjust their project implementation plans when necessary.

**3. Would I recommend this program to my friends? Why?**

I would be willing to recommend the FCAJ program to students who want to explore and develop their skills in information technology, especially in cloud computing and computer networking. The program provides opportunities to access new knowledge, practice real-world technologies, and develop independent learning and research skills.

### Suggestions and Expectations

**1. Suggestions for Improving the Internship Experience**

I hope the program will continue to provide practical project-based exercises with a learning path ranging from basic to advanced levels. Furthermore, additional in-depth training sessions on AWS, Hybrid Cloud, network security, and system monitoring would help interns strengthen their knowledge and improve their practical implementation skills.

**2. Interest in Continuing the Program in the Future**

I would welcome further opportunities to participate in FCAJ's learning activities and technical practice sessions in the future so that I can continue improving my knowledge of cloud computing, computer networking, and related technologies.

**3. Other Comments**

I would like to sincerely thank FCAJ for providing me with the opportunity to learn, practice, and gain valuable experience throughout my internship. The knowledge and experience I have gained will serve as an important foundation for my personal development, career orientation, and preparation for my future professional career.
